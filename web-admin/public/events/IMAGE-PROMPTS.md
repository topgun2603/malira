## `hero.png` — 2400 × 1400 (landscape, ~16:9)

> A wide cinematic photograph of an evening gathering in the Nilgiri hills:
> strings of warm festival lights strung between trees above an open ground,
> dusk sky in deep blue behind layered ridgelines, a few empty plastic chairs
> and a bunting line in the mid-ground. Shot from the back of the ground looking
> forward. Anticipation before a crowd arrives, not the crowd itself. Cool blue
> shadows against warm lamp light, gentle film grain, natural light, medium
> format. No identifiable faces, no text, no logos. Leave the left third calm
> for overlaid headline text.

**No identifiable people.** The hero sits above a public calendar; a
recognisable face there is a person who did not agree to be on the front of it.
